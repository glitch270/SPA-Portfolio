SPECIAL PROGRAM IN THE ARTS

MISSION, VISION AND OBJECTIVES

MISSION

To provide access to quality Basic Education thru enhancement of SPECIAL PROGRAM IN THE ARTS having Proficient and Skilled Teacher, Strong Manpower, Supportive Stakeholders and Far Reaching and Progressive School Facilities and Well Directed School Plan.

VISION

San Rafael National High School Special Program in the Arts aimed to develop young artists who will become globally competitive in the preservation of the Bicol/ Filipino culture and heritage through competent staff, excellent facilities and supportive stakeholders.

OBJECTIVES

  1. Acquire, improve and enhance students with special inclination to music, visual arts, theatre arts, dance, media arts and creative writing.
  2. Develop and hone students to express their ideas and feelings through their chosen art form.
  3. Cultivate and instill the Bicolano Spirit through deepened appreciation of Bicol culture and art forms.
  4. Widen the aesthetic awareness and perception particularly in the fourth district of Camarines Sur.
  5. Expound the general skills, attitudes, the habits of heart and mind of the students as preparation for life and work in a postmodern society regardless of their chosen career.
